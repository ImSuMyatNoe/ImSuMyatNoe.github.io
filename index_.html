About Me

I am a researcher with a Ph.D. in Machine Learning from the School of Computing, National University of Singapore (NUS). My research focuses on explainable AI (XAI) and human-AI collaboration, particularly in decision-making processes.

I am passionate about developing AI systems that are not only intelligent but also transparent, trustworthy, and aligned with human values. My work involves creating novel algorithms, conducting user studies, and building interactive systems to enhance human understanding and interaction with AI.
